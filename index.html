eBay Parts Automation
Privacy notice

6 November 2026

James Brady operates this private application to prepare and manage his own eBay parts listings. For privacy enquiries, contact drivewayquoter@gmail.com.

Information used and why

The project uses part photographs, descriptions, prices and listing records to prepare previews for seller approval. Once the seller connects an eBay account, the application uses only the eBay permissions granted. Depending on the features enabled, this can include account and listing identifiers, selling policies, listing performance, sales status and buyer messages. Buyer or order information is used only where needed to manage a sale, fulfil an order or resolve an enquiry.

We use this information for our legitimate interests in managing our own sales and keeping the application secure; where relevant, to fulfil a purchase or meet applicable legal obligations. Listing publication requires the seller's approval. An eBay connection does not provide access to the seller's email inbox.

Service providers and access

The project uses eBay, Make, Airtable, Cloudflare, OpenAI and the seller's iCloud project storage for the relevant parts of this workflow. GitHub Pages hosts this public notice. Information is available to the seller and authorised helpers only as needed for their work, and to these providers under their applicable terms and privacy notices. These services may process information outside the UK. We do not sell personal information or use buyer details for unrelated marketing.

Security, retention and account deletion

Application credentials are kept in protected service settings. The deletion-notification receiver verifies eBay signatures and stores minimal request metadata and pseudonymous account references for matched requests. It does not store raw incoming notification bodies. Historical notification records and execution history remain in Make pending review.

Information is kept only while needed for the sale, support, records or a valid retention requirement. Deletion requests are reviewed against retained records and processed where required; receipt of a notification does not automatically erase all records. You can request review or removal using the contact above. The seller can revoke application access in eBay's third-party application settings.

Your choices

Where UK data protection rights apply, you may request access, correction or deletion, object to processing, or request restriction or portability. Some rights depend on the circumstances and records may need to be retained for a valid legal reason. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
